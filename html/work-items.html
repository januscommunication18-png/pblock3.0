Title

A Cycle in Project Block is like a sprint — a dedicated timeframe where your team focuses on completing specific work items. It helps you break down your project into manageable chunks with clear start and end dates so everyone knows what to work on and when it needs to be done.

To set up a Cycle, give it a name, pick a start and due date, and add the work items you want to complete in that window. Track progress on the Cycle's burndown chart and review what shipped when it ends. Keep Cycles short (1–2 weeks) so scope stays focused and momentum stays high.
