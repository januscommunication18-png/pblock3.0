Badges & chips

Convention: neutral bordered badges always carry an explicit bg-white so they stay legible on hovered rows and grey group headers. Editable badges (border-stroke, open a picker on click) vs display chips (border-line, static). Status badges use a soft tint. Long values truncate; extra items collapse to “+N”.

Popover picker

Floating list that opens from an editable badge — optional search, brand-hover rows, a check on the selected item. Single-select closes on pick; multi-select stays open and toggles. Opening one picker closes any other.

Date range picker

Compact daterangepicker-style range selector: a preset sidebar beside two months, brand endpoints with the range fill between. Presets apply on click; “Custom Range” lights up for a manual selection. Used in Create cycle / Create module and on editable date badges.

Section card

Bordered card grouping list rows under a collapsible header with a count (work item drawer sections, cycle work items). Rows are flush, divided by border-line, and highlight on hover.
